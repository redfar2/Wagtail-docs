Pawly theme documentation

Pawly is a warm, playful Shopify theme made for pet stores. It includes a shop-by-pet picker, a size finder with breed guidance, a "Best for" pet profile on product pages, and organic pebble and wave shapes.

Getting started

Install and preview

  1. Add Pawly from the Shopify Theme Store. It is added to Online Store → Themes as an unpublished theme.
  2. Click Customize to open the theme editor. Your products, collections and menus appear right away.
  3. When you're happy with it, click Publish.

Recommended first steps

  1. Logo and colors: Theme settings → Logo, then Colors. Pawly has 5 color schemes; every section has its own scheme picker.
  2. Menus: the header uses your main-menu (up to three levels). The footer has menu columns you choose per block.
  3. Home page: pick collections for Shop by pet, the collection list and the featured collection.
  4. Product page: add pet profile tags and size guides to the products that need them (see below).
  5. Shipping estimates: set your processing and delivery times in Theme settings → Shipping estimates.
Every section is hidden on the storefront when it has nothing to show, for example a size finder on a product without sizes. You can safely leave sections in the product template.

Product page

The Product information section has these blocks, which you can reorder or remove:

  • Eyebrow (product type, vendor, collection or custom text), Title, Rating, Price (with unit prices and installments).
  • Short description from the custom.short_description metafield or the start of the description.
  • Pet profile, Inventory status with a low-stock threshold, Variant picker with color swatches.
  • Buy buttons with quantity, dynamic checkout, gift card recipient form, and store pickup availability.
  • Reassurance lines (delivery estimate, returns, secure checkout), Collapsible tabs and Custom Liquid.
  • App blocks from your installed apps.

Below the buy box, the default product template includes Product benefits, Product versions, How it works, Size finder, Specs and FAQ, Good to know, Product reviews, and complementary and related products. Each is hidden when the product has no data for it.

The gallery supports images, videos, YouTube and Vimeo videos, and 3D models, with click-to-zoom.

Pet profile ("Best for")

The Pet profile block shows up to four rows, such as Pet, Size, Age and Activity. Each row shows, in order of priority:

  1. the row's Value setting, which you can connect to a product metafield in the theme editor; or
  2. the text after a matching product tag. The default prefixes are pet:, size:, age: and energy:.
pet:Dogs
size:Small to large dogs
age:Adult dogs
energy:Evening walkers

Rows without a value are hidden, and the whole block is hidden when no row has a value. You can change each row's label, icon and tag prefix.

Size finder

The Size finder shows size cards with ranges, a second unit, typical breeds and details. When sizes have a minimum and maximum, shoppers can type their pet's measurement and the matching size is highlighted.

Option 1: per product, with a metafield (recommended)

Create a product metafield custom.size_guide of type JSON:

{
  "heading": "Find the right fit",
  "measure": "Fits neck",
  "unit": "cm",
  "input_label": "Your dog's neck",
  "tip": "Measure where the collar sits, then add two fingers of space.",
  "sizes": [
    { "size": "S", "range": "30–40 cm", "alt": "12–16 in", "min": 30, "max": 40,
      "breeds": "Jack Russell, Dachshund", "details": ["Width 2.0 cm"] },
    { "size": "M", "range": "38–50 cm", "alt": "15–20 in", "min": 38, "max": 50,
      "breeds": "Beagle, Border Collie" }
  ]
}

Option 2: with section blocks

Add Size blocks to the Size finder section. To show them only on some products, enter a tag in Only show on products tagged (for example collar-sizes) and add that tag to those products. For different size charts per product type, use an alternate product template for each.

Collections and search

  • Collection pages show a banner (collection image, a fallback image, or a decorative banner), filters and sorting. Configure filters in the Search & Discovery app.
  • The search overlay shows predictive results as shoppers type, plus your popular searches.
  • Related and complementary products use Shopify product recommendations. Complementary products are chosen in Search & Discovery.

Pages, blog and account

Pawly includes page templates for About (page heading, image banner, your page content as "Our story", value cards, call to action), Contact (form with support email, hours and FAQ link) and FAQ (questions grouped into tabs). Assign them under Online Store → Pages → Template.

Blog posts support comments, and the customer account links use Shopify's customer accounts.

Apps and app blocks

  • Add app blocks inside the Product information, Featured product, Product reviews and Testimonials sections.
  • Review apps such as Judge.me or Okendo: add their block to Product reviews. Ratings on product cards appear when the app writes the reviews.rating metafield.
  • Subscription apps such as Shopify Subscriptions: add their widget block to Product information.

Languages and markets

  • Pawly includes English and French for all storefront text and for the theme editor.
  • To sell in more languages, add them in Settings → Languages, then translate your own content (products, collections, section text) with the free Translate & Adapt app.
  • Turn on the country/region and language selectors in the Header and Footer sections. Add countries in Settings → Markets.

FAQ

Why don't I see the size finder or pet profile on a product?

They only appear when the product has data: a custom.size_guide metafield or the size finder tag, and pet profile tags or values.

Some text is still in English after I added a language.

Text you typed into sections, and your products and collections, are your own content. Translate them in Translate & Adapt under Online Store theme.

How do I change the paw icon in the announcement bar?

Turn off Show paw icon in the Announcement bar section.

Can I use my own fonts?

Pick any font from the Shopify font library in Theme settings → Typography.
